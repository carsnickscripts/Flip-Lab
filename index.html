Tutorial / Controls

Mobile
PC / Keyboard
◀ ▶
MoveBottom-left. Run left and right. In the air they steer you a little.
JUMP
JumpTap to hop. On a blue pad it launches you high. On a wall it kicks you off. On a bar it makes you leap.
FLIP
FlipHold in the air to spin and tuck. Holding it while pressing the direction you face is a front flip; the opposite direction is a back flip. Let go to open up and slow down. On a bar it pumps your swing.
GRAB
Grab barsHold it near a bar to hang on, release to let go. Turn on "Tap GRAB to toggle" in Settings if you would rather tap once to grab and tap again to drop.
FIRE
FireShows up on levels with weapons. Auto-aim can be turned on or off in Settings. Reloading happens automatically when you run dry.
SLOW-MO
Slow-moTop-center button. Tap to toggle slow motion on and off.
PISTOL
Switch weaponTop-center button on weapon levels. Tap to cycle to the next weapon.
PAUSE
PauseTop-right. Pauses and opens the pause menu.
🔥
EmotesIn multiplayer rooms, tap an emoji to show it over your head to friends.
TIPS
Wall: touch a wall in the air, then JUMP to kick off it. Bars: Left/Right shimmy along the bar, FLIP pumps the swing, JUMP leaps. Land close to upright to stick it.
LAYOUT
Move the buttons wherever you like: Settings, then "Edit control layout". You can also change button size and left-handed mode there.

Tap Rebind to replace an action's keys with one new key, or Add key to give it an extra key. Press Esc to cancel. If you pick a key another action already uses, it moves to the new action.
